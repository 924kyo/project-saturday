# ADR-0004 — Original Fictional Programs

**Status:** Accepted

## Decision

Use fictional university programs built from broad college-football archetypes and composite cultural/system traits.

## Why

- original world ownership;
- recognizable genre context without requiring licenses;
- supports player-created long-term history;
- avoids a renamed-copy feel.

## Constraint

Do not reproduce distinctive real marks, exact traditions, rosters, stadiums, slogans, or confusingly close identities.
