# Non-goals

Unless the roadmap explicitly changes, Project Saturday is **not** trying to build:

- a full 3D action football game;
- direct control of all 11 players;
- a head-coach dynasty-management simulator;
- licensed real universities or exact replicas of them;
- realistic simulation of every current NCAA legal/regulatory detail;
- a daily life simulator with dozens of chores;
- real-money card gacha;
- multiplayer or competitive online play in the initial release;
- backend/cloud dependency for the first vertical slice;
- 96 fully simulated teams at maximum fidelity every frame;
- AI-generated final art or text at runtime as a core dependency.

These exclusions protect the core promise: a smooth one-player career RPG.
