# Glossary

- **Career / Run**: one player's college career from creation/recruiting to retirement/draft/ending.
- **MetaProfile**: persistent account-like progression across careers.
- **Alumni**: saved record of a completed career.
- **Program**: a fictional university football program.
- **Depth Chart**: ordered role hierarchy at a position.
- **Snap Share**: expected or actual share of relevant offensive/defensive snaps.
- **Coach Trust**: staff confidence in the player, affecting opportunity but not replacing talent.
- **Scheme Fit**: compatibility between a player's profile and program/playbook role.
- **Body**: short-term physical readiness/fatigue resource, 0–100.
- **Durability**: longer-term attribute affecting injury and workload resilience.
- **Skill Card**: equipable build modifier obtained through in-career randomized choices.
- **Breakthrough**: a skill-card offer event, normally three cards with one selected.
- **Key Snap**: game situation where the simulation pauses for a player-relevant football decision.
- **Tag**: stable machine-readable identity/state label used to qualify content.
- **Brand**: public/commercial visibility that influences NIL opportunities.
- **NIL**: in-fiction commercial endorsement and brand opportunities for the athlete.
- **Program Familiarity**: persistent meta knowledge earned by playing careers at a program.
- **Vertical Slice**: first end-to-end playable season with the core experience at near-final quality for one position.
