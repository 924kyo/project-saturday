# Product Vision

## One sentence

A fast-flowing, build-driven college-football career RPG where one athlete fights for playing time, builds a distinct identity, lives through a changing campus/program world, and eventually joins the player's persistent alumni history.

## Player promise

Every career should answer:

> Who was this player, what did they become, and why did this run feel different from the last one?

## What makes Project Saturday distinct

1. **Player perspective rather than coach perspective.**
2. **Skill-card builds change career management.**
3. **Depth chart and snap share are core progression.**
4. **Key snaps make football decisions meaningful without a full action-sports implementation.**
5. **Tag-driven events make identity and history matter.**
6. **Fictional-but-legible programs create a recognizable college-football world without relying on licensed schools.**
7. **Alumni/legacy meta progression turns separate careers into one personal football history.**
8. **Complex simulation stays mostly behind a low-friction weekly flow.**

## Target platforms

Primary:

- mobile browser / installable PWA

Supported:

- desktop browser

Later possibilities must not distort the initial web architecture.

## Business model assumption

Initial design assumes a premium/free prototype without real-money skill-card purchases. In-game randomized cards are gameplay progression, not monetized loot boxes.
