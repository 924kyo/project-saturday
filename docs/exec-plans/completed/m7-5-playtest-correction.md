### B1 — authoritative evidence, then pure frames

1. Inventory active and retained snap evidence in `games/types.ts`, `transitions.ts`, `qb.ts`, `rb.ts`, `cb.ts`, and the current session/week replay contracts. WR active snaps own clock, possession, down, distance, yard line and score; retained WR plays own score/stat deltas but do not retain their original clock/situation or earned clues. Added-position pending snaps own decisions and earned clues but no field/clock context; completed plays own position-specific outcomes, draws and skill effects. Their final score is generated by the owning engine, not a live drive score. Historical v1 weekly summaries lack full snap logs entirely.
2. Establish only the missing authoritative current context/retention needed by the board in game-core, with an explicit version boundary and exact migration tests. New context must be created before the decision and retained with its resolution, not inferred from a future outcome. Complete an already-started historical game under its original rules; migration must not fabricate context or reroll it. Protect literal reports, original source versions, career/world RNG separation, archive proofs and bounded replay. Any rule change needed for physically consistent field/score evidence must be documented and limited to the versioned owning engine, never a second UI simulator.
3. Define one detached, immutable frame contract with stable position/actor/decision/result IDs, optional explicit unavailable-context reasons, public context, earned clues, schematic geometry and real consequence evidence. Position adapters map their own evidence into this contract; do not construct a pretend WR athlete or coerce defensive stats into offensive fields. Future LB/EDGE adapters extend the contract rather than branching the renderer into another model.
4. Project preview/decision/result frames without RNG, mutation, storage writes, hidden probabilities or unearned defensive detail. Geometry is original schematic content, not observed player tracking; path drawing must not contradict actual targets, catches, turnovers, touchdowns or yards. Unknown defenders remain uncertain. Historical missing facts get a truthful text/diagram fallback, not fabricated quarter/down/drive summaries.

Acceptance: real source-to-frame tests for every current position, all decision families, low/high information, no target, no opportunity, touchdowns/turnovers/tackles, current and literal historical reloads. Identical evidence produces identical frames; projection cannot change RNG, source ownership or rewards. Strict validation rejects altered/missing required current context while accepting actual absent historical context.

Additional source audit during the final M7 native gate: WR `resolvePlay` calculates receiving yards/TD independently of the active yard line, then `resolveKeySnap` advances aggregate simulation immediately. QB/RB calculate yards, TD and fumble fields independently; their fumble field alone does not establish a saved loss/recovery location. CB calculates completion yardage, tackle and TD-allowed evidence independently. These are existing abstract contracts, not tracked trajectories. Before drawing current absolute endpoints, pin representative literal cases and specify the smallest versioned owning-engine extension needed for coherent pre/post field bounds, scoring and possession evidence. Do not move a pre-snap line after learning the outcome, infer a lost ball from an ambiguous historical fumble field, or silently alter existing game/report bytes. This is a required B1 compatibility/physics audit, not live M7 rule activation.
