# Draft and Career Endings

## Naming/licensing

Use **Pro Draft** and fictional professional framing by default. Use of real league branding requires separate legal/licensing review and is outside the autonomous default.

## Draft stock dimensions

Draft evaluation can consider:

- athletic traits/size;
- position skill ratings;
- production;
- level of competition/program exposure;
- big-game performance;
- durability/injury history;
- awards;
- consistency;
- combine/pro-day style evaluation where implemented.

Avoid making Overall alone determine draft result.

## Declaration

When eligible in the fictional ruleset, the player may face a decision:

- declare for pro draft;
- return for another college season, if career eligibility remains.

Show projected range with uncertainty, not guaranteed exact pick.

## End states

Possible endings include:

- drafted early;
- drafted late;
- undrafted/pro opportunity abstracted;
- college career complete without pro path;
- career-ending/retirement story;
- user chooses to end career when appropriate.

Every ending produces an Alumni record.

## Final career presentation

Show:

- program(s);
- seasons;
- stats;
- awards;
- championships;
- best games/records;
- signature skill build;
- draft/ending;
- persistent legacy rewards/unlocks.

The ending should feel like closing a character story, not merely returning to title screen.
