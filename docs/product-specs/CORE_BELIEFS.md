# Core Beliefs

> **1.0 RC:** what ships is summarized in `docs/release/SPEC_CONFORMANCE.md` and specified by `docs/product-reconciliation/CAREER_VNEXT_CONTRACT.md`. Milestone-numbered sections (M3–M7.5) describe the pre-R aggregates, which are kept only for historical saves, replays and reports.

These are product invariants. A feature that violates them needs an explicit design decision, not accidental implementation.

## 1. Flow is the highest priority

A normal week should feel like:

> context → 3 meaningful focus choices → event when relevant → game/key snaps → consequences → next week

The mandatory team schedule is implicit. Body, Preparation, and Confidence create choices, not three recurring chores. Do not make the player visit eight management screens to keep systems healthy.

## 2. Skill builds should alter decisions

The best card is not merely `Speed +3`. Good cards change the relative value of training, recovery, information, risk, relationships, playing time, or game decisions.

## 3. Opportunity is progression

Depth rank, projected snaps, role, coach trust, awards, brand, and draft stock are emotionally stronger than isolated +1 attributes. Surface those changes prominently.

## 4. Careers need identity

Background, personality, appearance, program, skills, relationships, and tags should alter available events and strategic choices.

The athlete must also be visually present. Creation choices produce a persistent graphical identity used throughout the career, not only a list of saved text fields.

## 5. Simulate deeply; expose selectively

The engine may track detailed probabilities. The player sees only what improves a decision. Advanced details can live behind info affordances.

## 6. Failure produces stories, not dead runs

Bench careers, transfers, injuries, or undrafted endings still contribute legacy discovery and persistent history.

## 7. Meta progression expands the possibility space

Unlock options, information, cards, cosmetics, and alumni interactions. Avoid compounding permanent stat boosts that erase early-career tension.

## 8. Fictional programs must feel real through systems

Culture and identity should emerge from program attributes, traits, recruiting footprint, schemes, fan pressure, rivals, presentation, and history—not copied trademarks.

## 9. Bilingual quality is product quality

Korean and English are not separate projects. Every screen and content item is complete only when both supported locales are valid.

## 10. Every role participates

Playing time remains scarce and earned, but a bench athlete is not a spectator to an empty screen. Every scheduled game should produce truthful role-appropriate participation, learning, or feedback without fabricating offensive stats.
