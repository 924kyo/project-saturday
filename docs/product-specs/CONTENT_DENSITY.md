# Content Density Targets

Quantity follows systems, not the reverse.

## Vertical slice (WR, 12 programs, one season)

Target ranges:

- 12 fictional programs
- 1 playable position (WR)
- 3 WR archetypes
- 5 recruiting backgrounds
- 8 personality traits
- approximately 40 skill cards across Development, Role/Coach, Game Day, Body, Mindset, and Life
- 50–70 events
- 9+ weekly action definitions
- 8–12 NIL offer templates only if NIL is in the target milestone
- 8–12 injury/recovery outcomes if injuries are enabled
- 6–10 key-snap decision patterns
- basic regular season + postseason path
- complete ko-KR/en-US copy for all shipping content

Current M5 skill coverage is exactly 40 original bilingual WR cards across all six target families (8 Development / 7 Role-Coach / 10 Game Day / 5 Body / 6 Mindset / 4 Life). The 13 additive M5 cards preserve all 27 prior IDs/effects, produce 13 validated tradeoffs, and add live weekly, Game Day, event, and injury consequences.

Current M4 game coverage is 12 fictional opponent projections, 4 WR decision families, 12 stable athlete-level choices, 7 coverage/leverage clues, 8 original bilingual key-snap patterns, and 6 bilingual participation-feedback categories (one offensive, five non-offensive). This satisfies the first-game pattern target without copying names, situations, rules, or writing from another game.

Current M5 event coverage is 57 original bilingual events and 114 choices. Category unions provide 20 WR/depth, 16 identity/background/personality, and 22 program/game-context events. Eligibility combines authored player identity, equipped skill hooks, season/venue/spotlight context, program traits, bounded current state, density, and cooldowns so an individual career sees a contextual subset rather than the whole catalog.

The staged M7 QB vertical meets its added-position minimum with exactly 12 mechanically live skills, 12 position-sensitive events, 8 key-snap patterns, 12 athlete-level choices, and 24 situational clues. Every definition and event choice has original paired ko-KR/en-US copy. These entries are additive to the preserved WR catalogs and do not count toward live shipping totals until the full multi-position persistence/UI boundary activates.

The staged M7 RB vertical independently meets the same minimum: 12 mechanically live skills, 12 RB events, 8 patterns, 12 decisions, and 24 clues with complete paired ko-KR/en-US copy. Its content covers rushing track, contact/security, protection, and receiving responsibilities rather than reusing renamed QB/WR patterns.

The staged M7 CB vertical also ships 12 mechanically live skills, 12 CB events, 8 patterns, 12 decisions, and 24 clues with paired ko-KR/en-US copy. Its leverage, coverage, ball, and tackle content completes the added-position alpha density without copying offensive layouts, text, data, or rules.

- 100–130+ skill cards

- 40–60 NIL deal types/templates

## Reuse philosophy

Prefer conditional recombination over 1,000 generic events.

A 300-event library where each career sees a context-dependent subset is better than 1,000 events that ignore identity.
